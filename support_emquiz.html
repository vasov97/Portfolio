Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn coins?

You can earn coins by completing daily login rewards or by watching rewarded video ads. Coins can be used to purchase hints, skips, and other power-ups to help you progress!

What are hints and skips?

Hints reveal part of the answer to help you when you're stuck. Skips let you bypass a difficult question and move to the next one. Both cost coins but can save your streak!

How does the daily reward work?

Log in every day to collect free coins! The more consecutive days you log in, the bigger your reward. Don't break your streak!

How are rankings calculated?

Global rankings are based on your total points earned from correctly answering questions. Harder questions award more points. Compete with Eurovision fans worldwide!

How do I change my avatar?

Go to your profile settings and tap on your current avatar. Choose from our collection of kawaii-style avatars to personalize your account!

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the settings menu on the home screen. Alternatively, email us at vasovmail@gmail.com and we'll delete your account within 48 hours.

I found a bug, how do I report it?

Please email us at vasovmail@gmail.com with details about the bug, including your device model and iOS/Android version. We appreciate your help!

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here's what data we collect and how we use it:

  • We collect your email address for authentication via Firebase
  • We use AdMob for ads which may collect device identifiers
  • We store game progress (points, coins, level) in Firebase Firestore
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • Contact vasovmail@gmail.com for data deletion requests

Account Deletion

You have full control over your account and data. We respect your right to be forgotten.

How to Delete Your Account:

Option 1: Open the app and go to Settings on the home screen. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm your choice.

Option 2: Email us at vasovmail@gmail.com with "Delete Account" in the subject line. We'll process your request within 48 hours.

⚠️ Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your progress, coins, and rankings will be lost.
