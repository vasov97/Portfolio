About the Game

Flipside is a neon-styled endless runner where you tap to flip gravity and avoid obstacles. Challenge yourself to beat your high score in this fast-paced arcade game!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere on the screen to flip gravity and avoid obstacles. The game gets faster as you progress!

How do I revive after dying?

Watch a rewarded video ad to continue your run from where you left off.

How can I report a bug?

Please email us at vasovmail@gmail.com with details about the issue, including your device model and Android/iOS version.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely! We'd love to hear your ideas. Send us an email with your suggestions.
