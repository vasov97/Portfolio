About the Game

Dragon Paws is a jigsaw puzzle adventure for kids ages 3-8. Meet 27 adorable dragon chihuahua pups, solve 250+ colorful puzzles, read original comics, and collect stickers along the way!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Drag and drop puzzle pieces into place to complete each dragon's picture. Puzzles range from easy 2x2 grids to challenging 4x5 grids.

How do I unlock more dragons?

The first 5 dragons are free to play. Unlock the rest by upgrading to Dragon Paws Club, or earn them through special packs and daily rewards.

What are hints and how do I get them?

Hints highlight where a puzzle piece belongs. Earn hint tokens by reaching puzzle milestones, or spend coins to use one anytime.

How does the daily reward wheel work?

Check in every day to build your streak. Reach a 7-day streak to spin the reward wheel and win coins, hints, dragon shards, or special packs.

How can I report a bug?

Please email us at vasovmail@gmail.com with details about the issue, including your device model and Android/iOS version.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely! We'd love to hear your ideas. Send us an email with your suggestions.

Is Dragon Paws safe for kids?

Yes. Dragon Paws requires no account or login, collects no personal data from children, and is designed for safe, independent play.
