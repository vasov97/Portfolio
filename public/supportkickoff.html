Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Pick a nickname and flag, then tap Kick Off to start answering timed football trivia questions. Earn points and coins for correct answers.

What are coins for?

Coins are an in-app currency you earn by playing. Spend them on power-ups like 50/50, Skip, Reveal, and Buy Time during a quiz.

How does the daily quiz work?

Every day there are 5 fresh questions for a bonus reward. Come back daily to keep your check-in streak alive.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Answer questions correctly to earn points. The top 30 players worldwide appear on the global leaderboard.

Is an account required?

No. Just pick a nickname and flag - no email, password, or login needed.
