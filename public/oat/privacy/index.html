Privacy Policy

OAT – Osteoarthritis Tracker · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: OAT stores your diary so you can track your joints and share reports with your doctor. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. AI features receive your diary entries without your name or email. You can export or permanently delete all your data in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

OAT ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile app for Android and iOS that helps people with osteoarthritis and joint pain track their symptoms. It is developed and operated by Stefan Vasov (Magma Dev). For any privacy question or request, contact us at vasovmail@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Account data. When you sign in with Google (or Apple), we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL and an account identifier. See section 3 for how we handle Google user data.

Health and diary data you enter. This includes your health profile (affected joints, symptom duration, typical pain, challenges and goal), daily check-ins (pain, stiffness, sleep, activity, mood and notes), flares (joints, severity, possible triggers, notes and times), monthly joint checks (your chosen activities and ratings, joint ratings and change ratings), and appointments you add.

Weather data (optional). If you turn on weather in the app, your phone's approximate location is used once, right after you save a check-in, to look up the local weather. The location is rounded to about 1 km and is never stored. Only the weather values (temperature, air pressure, humidity, rain and a weather description) are saved with your check-in.

App settings. Your reminder settings, weather setting, and which articles you have read.

Subscription data. If you subscribe to OAT Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive your subscription status (for example, active or expired) but never your card or payment details.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent so we can fix it: the error, the app version, your device model and operating system. These reports do not include your diary entries, name or email.

3. Google user data

OAT offers "Sign in with Google". When you choose it, Google shares the following data with OAT, with your permission:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your profile picture URL, and
  • a unique Google account identifier.

OAT only requests these basic profile permissions (openid, email and profile). OAT does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, location history or any other Google data.

How we use Google user data: only to create and identify your OAT account, sign you in securely, show your name and email in the app, and optionally add your name to the PDF reports you create.

How we store it: in our secure database hosted by Supabase, protected so that only your account can access it.

How we share it: we do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. It is not sent to our AI provider. It is only processed by the service providers that run the app on our behalf (section 7), as needed to provide sign-in.

Deletion: when you delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), your Google user data is permanently deleted from our database. You can also remove OAT's access at any time in your Google Account settings, under "Third-party apps and services".

OAT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the app: saving your diary, showing your trends, history and statistics, and syncing across your devices.
  • To create AI insights, flare reflections and report summaries, when you ask for them (see section 5).
  • To create PDF reports, which are generated on your phone and only shared when you choose to.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock Premium features.
  • To fix bugs and keep the app reliable.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and do not send data to us. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. AI features

When you request an AI insight, a flare reflection or a summary for your doctor, the relevant diary entries (for example, the last 30 days of check-ins, flares, weather and your health profile answers) are sent to OpenAI to generate the text. Your name, email and other Google user data are not sent. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default. AI-generated text describes possible patterns in your own data; it is not a diagnosis and not medical advice.

6. Health data and your consent

Your diary contains health information, which privacy laws treat as sensitive. We only process it with your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 9).

7. Who processes your data for us

We use trusted service providers that process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase – secure database, sign-in and server functions (stores your account and diary).
  • OpenAI – generates AI insights and summaries (diary entries only, without your name, email or Google user data).
  • MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute) – weather data, receiving only rounded coordinates, without any personal information.
  • RevenueCat – manages subscriptions (your account identifier and subscription status).
  • Google and Apple – sign-in and in-app purchases.
  • Sentry – crash and error reports (technical data only).

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account, your Google user data and all diary data are deleted permanently from our database. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short period before they are overwritten. Crash reports are kept for a limited time by Sentry and then deleted automatically.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: export all your data at any time in the app (Profile → Export my data).
  • Correction: edit your check-ins, flares, appointments and health profile in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account and all your data in the app (Profile → Delete account), or see how to delete your account without the app.
  • Weather and reminders: turn these on or off at any time in the app.
  • Other requests or complaints: contact us at vasovmail@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your diary is protected so that only your account can access it, your sign-in session is encrypted on your phone, and AI keys and payment checks are kept on our servers, never in the app.

11. Children

OAT is intended for adults (18 and older) and is not directed at children.

12. Not medical advice

OAT is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical advice. If you have new, severe or worrying symptoms, contact a doctor or your local emergency number.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
