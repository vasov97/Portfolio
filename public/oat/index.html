OAT – Osteoarthritis Tracker

OAT – Osteoarthritis Tracker is a mobile app that helps people with osteoarthritis and joint pain keep a simple health diary, understand what affects their symptoms and flares, and share a clear report with their doctor or physiotherapist.

What the app does

  • Daily check-ins – record pain, stiffness, sleep, activity and mood in about 30 seconds.
  • Flare tracking – log flares, possible triggers and how long they last.
  • Trends and patterns – charts, a pain calendar, and optional local weather to see what affects your joints.
  • Monthly joint check – short questions to follow how your joints change over time.
  • Doctor report – a PDF summary of your diary to share before an appointment.
  • AI insights – optional summaries of patterns in your own diary entries.
  • Reminders and articles – gentle check-in reminders and evidence-based information about osteoarthritis.

How OAT uses Google Sign-In

OAT lets you create an account with "Sign in with Google". OAT only receives your basic profile information (name, email address and profile picture) and uses it solely to create your account, sign you in securely and keep your diary private to you. OAT does not access any other Google data, does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Who it is for

Adults living with osteoarthritis or joint pain who want to understand their symptoms better and have more informed conversations with their healthcare professionals.

OAT is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical advice.
